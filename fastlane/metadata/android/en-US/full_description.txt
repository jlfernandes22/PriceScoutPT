Compare prices at Continente, Lidl, Pingo Doce, Aldi and Auchan in one place, even without an internet connection.

PriceScoutPT downloads the catalog to your phone (offline-first) and lets you:

• Search products by name, with supermarket and category filters
• Build your shopping basket and see which supermarket is cheapest
• Check the real price history of the last 30 days, to avoid fake promotions
• Update the catalog with a single gesture (pull-to-refresh or sync button)

Prices are collected automatically every day. They are national reference prices (online catalog) - prices in physical stores may vary by region.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Your data stays only on your device.
